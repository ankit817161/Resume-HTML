Classic Choclate Chip Cookies 🍪

  • A timeless treat by loved all! 💖

    • Ingredients 📝

    Dry Ingredients 🧂

    • 2 1/4cups all purpose flour
    • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
    • 1 teaspoon salt

    Wet Ingredients 🧈

    • 1 cup unsalted butter
    • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
    • 1 cup packed brown sugar
    • 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
    • 2 large eggs

    Mix-ins 🍫

    • 2 cups semisweet and/or milk choclate chips
    • Optional: 1/2 cup chopped walnuts or pecans

    Instructions 🍽

    1. Preheat your oven to 350°C
    2. In a small bowl,whisk together the flour and baking soda;set aside.🥄
    3. In a large bowl,beat the butter with both sugars untilcreamy and light in texture.🧈🍬
    4. Add the salt,vanilla extract,and eggs,one at a time,beating after each addition.🧂🍦🥚
    5. Gradually add the flour mixture to the wet ingredients,mixing just until combined.Be careful not to overmix.🍚
    6. Fold the chocolate chips(and nuts,if using)until evenly distributed.🍪🍫
    7. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough on to the prepared baking sheet,spacing them about 2 inches apart.🍽
    8. Bake for 10-12 minutes,or until the ages are golden brown,but the centers are still soft.🍪⏲
      • Note: For chewier cookies,remove from oven when still slightly undercooked.For crispier cookies,bake an additional minute.🍪❤
    9. Allow the cookies to cool on the baking sheet for 2 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.⏱🍪

    Tips and Variations 💡

    • Substitute the Butter🧈:Use a coconut oil for a dairy-free option. 🥥
    • Gluten-Free🍶:Replace the all-purpose flour with a gluten-free flour blend. 🍶🚫
    • More Choclate🍫:Add extra chocolate chips or even drizzle melted chocolate over the finished cookies for a richer taste.🍪🍫

    Frequently Asked Questions ❓

    Can i freeze the cookie dough?❄
      Yes! You can freeze the dough for upto 3 months.Roll it into balls and freeze them on a baking sheet,then store them in a freezer bag.🧊🍪
How can i make the cookies thicker?🍪
      Chill the doughin the fridge for 30 minutes before baking. This helps the cookies hold their shapeand prevents them from spreding too much.🧊🍪
What if I don't have brown sugar?🍬
      You can substitute granulated sugar with 1/2 cup of white sugar 1/2 cup of molasses to make your own brown sugar.🍬🍟
